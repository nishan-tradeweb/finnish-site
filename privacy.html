Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

1. Who we are

finnish is a screenplay-writing app and website created and operated by Nishan, India. In this policy, “we” means Nishan. You can reach us by email at mailstonishan@gmail.com.

2. Your scripts stay on your device

Everything you write or import is saved only on your phone, tablet or computer. We do not receive a copy and we cannot read it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your scripts are gone unless you made a backup or an export.

3. Your account

You need an account to use finnish. We store your email address, a sign-in record, the date your free trial started, your plan and its end date, and the country used to set your price. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Your account is kept with Google Firebase, our account provider. One account works on every platform we support.

4. Region and VPN check

To keep prices fair, our server looks at your internet address (IP address) to find your country and to detect a VPN or proxy. The IP address is checked with a detection service and is not kept in your account record. A VPN never blocks the app. It only pauses new subscriptions until you turn it off.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Microsoft Store. We never see your card or bank details. We receive only the subscription status from the store.

6. Backup to Google Drive

If you choose Back up to Google Drive, your backup file is saved in a hidden folder that only finnish can open. We never see it. You can delete it in your Google Account settings.

7. Features that use an internet connection

Most of finnish works offline. These features contact outside services, and only when you use them:

  • Full-script translation sends the text you choose to translate to online translation services, such as Google Translate and Microsoft Translator. If one is unavailable, the app may use another provider.
  • Spelling suggestions in Proofread send the words being checked to Google.
  • Scan to Script reads photographed pages on your device. The first time you use a language, it downloads a text-recognition language pack from a public network. Your pictures are not uploaded.
  • Voice typing, when you use it, is handled by the speech service of your device or browser, which may send audio to its provider.
  • Some optional fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when you choose them.

These services have their own privacy policies. Do not use these features for text you must keep confidential.

8. This website

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website. It does the following:

  • It saves your choice of theme (Matte White or Matte Dark) and language in your browser’s local storage, so the site remembers them. This stays on your device.
  • It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google and your IP address is visible to it.
  • It asks Cloudflare and country.is which country you are in, so it can show a fair local price. The answer is used on the page and is not stored by us.
  • If you install the site as an app, or use it offline, it stores copies of its own pages and images on your device.

9. No ads, no tracking

The app has no advertising and no analytics.

10. How long we keep data

We keep your account record while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete the record from our server.

11. Your rights and choices

You can see your account details in Account & plan. You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time in the app, under Account & plan, then Delete account. You can clear this website’s stored settings from your browser’s site-data settings.

Depending on where you live, for example the EU, the UK, India or California, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it. Write to mailstonishan@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

12. Children

finnish is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law requires. We do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes

If we make a significant change to this policy, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mailstonishan@gmail.com.
