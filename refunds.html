Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: 2026-11-24

1. Free trial

The 5-day free trial is free. Nothing is charged during the trial.

2. Cancelling

Cancel renewal in the subscription settings of the store you used: Google Play, App Store or Microsoft Store. You will not be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

3. Refunds

Your payment is taken and refunded by the store you bought from, under that store’s own refund rules. To ask for a refund, use the store’s “Report a problem” or refund request page. Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store each have one. We cannot refund a store payment ourselves, but if you need help with a request, write to mailstonishan@gmail.com.

4. Your scripts

Cancelling never deletes your scripts. You can always open, read and export them.
